CLOSED-LOOP OPERATING PROCESS
1
STEP 01
HEAT REJECTION
Heat rejection is the transfer of collected thermal energy from the facility loop to an external sink without mixing the two fluids.
→
2
STEP 02
PRIMARY LOOP
The primary cooling loop is the closed fluid path that carries heat from compute cold plates to a facility heat exchanger.
→
3
STEP 03
SENSE & CONTROL
Temperature, flow, and differential-pressure sensors feed a local controller that adjusts pump speed and valve position every control interval.
→
4
STEP 04
COLD-PLATE INTAKE
Stage 1 moves coolant through cold plates where server heat enters the primary loop.
